# Voice

The composer takes speech. **Dictation**: the microphone button next to the
paperclip (on the new-session page and in a session's reply box) starts
listening; words appear in the box as they are recognized, interim ones
included; pressing it again (it is a Stop button while listening, labelled
"Stop dictation") or Escape stops, and the text stays in the box to edit.
Dictation goes after what is already typed. **Voice mode** (the waveform
button next to it, on the new-session page and in a session's reply box): says "Voice mode on.",
listens, sends after 1.5 s of silence, reads the turn's last answer aloud
(Markdown stripped, code blocks and tables named, not read), then listens
again. An approval is announced ("Needs your approval: ...") and never
answered by voice: the mic stays off until it is decided on screen, then the
turn's answer is read. Talking over an answer stops it and starts the next
message; an echo of the answer through the speakers does not; "stop" said over
it only stops it. The bar over the text says what voice mode is doing, with
Stop (while reading) and Turn off. Escape stops dictation, then the reading,
then voice mode. A browser without speech recognition shows the microphone
greyed out (`aria-disabled`) with the reason in its tooltip and, when pressed,
under the box; voice mode stays visible with `aria-disabled` and explains the same limitation. A blocked microphone says so under
the box and leaves both off.

Headless Chromium has no microphone and no voices, so every recipe replaces
`SpeechRecognition`/`webkitSpeechRecognition` with a recorder whose results
the steps feed through `window.__say(text, final)` (`window.__recs`, the
instances; `window.__live`, the one listening), and `speechSynthesis` with
one that records what it is asked to say (`window.__said`), counts cancels
(`window.__hush`) and ends each utterance after `window.__speakMs` ms. The
recognition stub is read when the microphone is pressed, so it can be
installed after the page loads.

## Setup

```sh
yv iso up
yv iso seed
```

## Cleanup

```sh
yv iso down
```

read, an echo of it ("echo test barge one") is ignored, the user's own words
("TEST-wait actually") silence it and go in the box. Stop silences a reading;

## Gotchas
- The stubs stand in for the browser's speech engines: real recognition,
  real voices, the permission prompts and echo cancellation through real
  speakers are not exercised here (see docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, Voice).
- The desktop app (WKWebView) cannot run here; its microphone and speech
  permissions are only checked on a Mac.
